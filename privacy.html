Page to Markdown for AI

Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-23

Page to Markdown for AI does not collect, sell, or share your browsing data or page content.

  • Page content is read only when you click the extension, use its keyboard shortcut, or choose it from the right-click menu, and only on the tab you are using.
  • All conversion to Markdown happens locally in your browser. No page content is sent to any server.
  • Your settings (title/URL toggle, prompt template) are stored with Chrome's built-in storage.sync.
  • If you buy Pro, your license key is sent to our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy (api.lemonsqueezy.com), to activate and periodically verify it. The key and the purchase email returned by Lemon Squeezy are stored locally in your browser. No page content is ever sent.
  • The extension contains no analytics, trackers, or advertising.
